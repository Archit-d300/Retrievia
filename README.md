# Retrievia 📚🧠

**Retrievia** is an AI-powered **Corrective Retrieval-Augmented Generation (CRAG)** platform that helps students learn more efficiently from textbooks, notes, research papers, and previous-year question papers.

Built using **Next.js**, **ShadCN UI**, **FastAPI**, **LangChain**, and **LangGraph**, Retrievia enables users to upload study materials and instantly gain actionable insights through intelligent document retrieval, AI-powered summarization, topic discovery, and contextual question answering.

Rather than forcing students to manually search through hundreds of pages, Retrievia identifies the exact concepts, chapters, and sections relevant to a query while enriching responses with additional information from trusted web sources.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Document Intelligence

* Upload textbooks, notes, PDFs, research papers, and question papers
* Automatic document parsing and indexing
* Semantic understanding of educational content

### 🧠 AI-Powered Summaries

* Chapter-wise summaries
* Topic-level breakdowns
* Revision notes
* Quick exam preparation guides

### 🔍 Context-Aware Question Answering

Ask questions naturally:

> "Explain deadlock prevention."

> "Where is Dijkstra's Algorithm discussed in my notes?"

> "Which topics are repeatedly asked in previous-year papers?"

Retrievia retrieves the most relevant information and generates grounded responses using your study material.

### 📊 Question Paper Analysis

* Identify frequently asked concepts
* Discover important chapters
* Analyze topic trends
* Estimate topic weightage

### 🎯 Smart Topic Recommendations

Retrievia pinpoints:

* Relevant chapters
* Exact sections
* Related concepts
* Supporting resources

Helping students focus on what matters most.

### 🌐 Hybrid Knowledge Retrieval

Combines information from:

* Uploaded documents
* User notes
* Previous-year papers
* Trusted online educational resources

to provide comprehensive and up-to-date answers.

### 🔄 Corrective RAG (CRAG)

Unlike traditional RAG systems, Retrievia:

* Evaluates retrieval quality
* Corrects weak retrieval results
* Reduces hallucinations
* Improves factual grounding
* Enhances answer reliability
